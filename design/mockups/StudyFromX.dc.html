Has Calgary rent outpaced wages since 2020?

Yes. Typical rent rose about 38% while typical wages rose about 17%, so rent now takes a bigger bite of every paycheque.

MethodSourcesDownload data

RENT+38%median 2-bed

HARDEST-HIT AREANW+26 pt gap

What I found

  1. 01Rent grew 38%, wages 17%. The gap opened in 2022, not 2020.
  2. 02After inflation, rent is up 15% and wages are down 2%.
  3. 03The NW saw the widest gap, at 26 points.

WHAT THIS DOESN'T SHOW

These are asking rents for vacant units. People who stayed in the same place usually pay less, so the squeeze is sharpest for movers.

Method

Rental Market SurveyCMHC · 2020–2025 · Open licencesource ↗
Average weekly earningsStatistics Canada · 2020–2025 · Open Government Licencesource ↗
Steps
  • Pulled and cleaned both datasets
  • Matched geography and time periods
  • Indexed both to 2020 = 100
  • Adjusted for inflation with Alberta CPI
Assumptions and limits
  • Wages are Alberta-wide; rent is Calgary only
  • Asking rents, not what sitting tenants pay
  • A bigger wage jump in 2026 would narrow the gap
