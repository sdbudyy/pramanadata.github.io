PART 7

Spot the trick

A real-looking headline. What's wrong with it?

{{feedbackTitle}}

Going from 2 to 8 is +300%, but it's 6 extra incidents on one street. With a base this small, percentages make random noise look like a trend. The honest version: “6 more break-ins than last year; too few to call a trend.”
